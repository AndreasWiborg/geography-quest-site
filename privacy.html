What we store

On your device: your progress (lessons, stamps, gems, streak, settings), your reminder time, and downloaded voice packs. On our servers (Supabase, EU region): the anonymous player id, generated name, level, XP, stamps, streak, hat, home country, time zone, reminder settings, league group, friends, clan membership and clan chat selections (preset messages only), challenge and duel rounds, and a push notification token if you turn reminders on. If you sign in with Apple we store the Apple user identifier; we do not store your email or real name.

Product analytics

For players 13 and over we use PostHog (EU servers) to understand which lessons and games work: events such as "lesson completed" and "game finished", the screens visited, and session recordings of the app's screens. Nothing personal appears on screen (names are generated, there is no free text). You can turn analytics off at any time in Friends > settings. Under-13 players are opted out before any data is sent.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive the purchase status (for example "Plus active") and never see payment details.

Notifications

With your permission we send reminders. The server decides when to send them from your own app activity (when you usually play) and never while you are likely asleep. Turn them off any time in iOS Settings or in the app.

Children

The app is designed for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: the app asks the player's age band at the start and disables analytics for under-13 players. Parents can delete a child's account from Friends > settings or by emailing us.

Your rights

Email support@kawisoftware.com to access, correct or delete your data. Account deletion in the app removes your server data immediately.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes what it collects, with the date above.
